# Chem 1151 study guide

An interactive study guide for Chem 1151 (General Chemistry, Prof. Dransfield). It covers every section of Gilbert, *Chemistry: An Atoms-Focused Approach* (3rd ed.), Chapters 1–4, and the Day 1–8 lectures: 41 sections, 458 problems, and interactive models. Each section teaches the idea first, then asks you to use it. Hints come one step at a time, and solutions stay hidden until you open them.

## Open the guide

1. Click the green **Code** button near the top of this page, choose **Download ZIP**, and unzip it. (Or `git clone` the repository.)
2. Open `study-guides/CurrentCourseGuide/index.html` in a web browser by double-clicking it. It works offline, and there's nothing to install.
3. Your answers, hints used, and progress are saved in that browser only. **Reset progress** in the sidebar clears them.

To jump straight to a section, add its code to the end of the address, for example `index.html#m19` for Lewis structures. Each section's code is in the tables below.

## How each section works

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Learn | the big idea, told three ways: what you observe, what you write, and what the particles are doing |
| Understand | why it works, what every symbol means, and which look-alike it's easy to confuse it with |
| Explore | an interactive model: change one thing and predict what happens before you look |
| Attempt | one problem before any worked example, with hints one at a time |
| Compare | the tempting wrong reasoning next to the correct reasoning |
| Practice | problems with less and less help |
| Master | a transfer problem, an explanation in your own words, a recognition question, and a sanity check |

## What the labels mean

- **Covered in lecture**: taught on the Day 1–8 slides. Each block cites the slide, for example “Day 8 p.21.”
- **Textbook preview**: in the textbook but not yet taught in lecture. It's there for reading ahead; whether it will be on an exam isn't known.
- **Lecture + preview**: a lecture topic with a boxed textbook-preview part.

## What's not included

The lecture slides and the textbook aren't in this repository; they aren't ours to share. The guide cites both by page, for example “Day 8 p.21” or “textbook §4.4, PDF p.197 (printed 163),” so you can follow along in your own copies.
