Significant figures in one place

Textbook preview The lecture has no written policy, but its worked answers carry the precision of the least-precise input (Day 1 p.11; Day 4 p.12, p.14). The textbook's rules (§1.7, PDF p.57–59): leading zeros never count; captive zeros always count; trailing zeros after a decimal point count; × and ÷ keep the fewest significant figures; + and − keep the fewest decimal places; exact numbers never limit; round only at the end.
