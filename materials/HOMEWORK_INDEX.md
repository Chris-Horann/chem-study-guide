## Conventions

- Cite `homework/<file> p.N, problem #` for every entry.
- Label entries as in `COURSE_INDEX.md` (SOURCE-DERIVED, INFERRED, …).
- Difficulty scale: **1** single-step recall/plug-in · **2** one concept, several
  steps · **3** two concepts combined or reversed givens · **4** multi-concept, the
  method must be recognized · **5** transfer to an unfamiliar context.
- "Professor-specific expectations" require evidence (instructions, rubrics,
  answer formats, online-system tolerances); otherwise label INFERRED.
