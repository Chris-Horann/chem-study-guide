# Course Index

Persistent cumulative understanding of lecture slides, notes, review material, and other course sources.

Organize primarily by concept rather than by filename.

For each topic, preserve:
- source filename
- original page/slide number
- topic and subtopics
- definitions
- equations and relationships
- macroscopic interpretation
- symbolic representation
- particle-level explanation
- professor explanations
- worked examples
- diagrams and graphs
- recognition cues
- problem-solving techniques
- common mistakes
- explicit emphasis
- prerequisites
- connections
- uncertainties

Do not claim professor emphasis unless directly supported by the supplied materials.
